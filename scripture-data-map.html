Scripture Data Map

One SQLite database. Every word of all four works gets a permanent number, and every tag points at a run of those numbers or at an entity. Counts are live from scripture.db as of the latest build.

Built by script Script part built, AI part waiting Waiting on AI No table yet

A passage is a range of word numbers

Word ids follow reading order inside an edition. So any highlight, from one word to a whole sermon, is a first and last word id. Tags never store text. They store ranges.

Every layer points at words, entities, or both

Two hubs hold everything else up. Each layer in the middle is its own set of tables, and each can be rebuilt or dropped without touching another. Line labels name what the layer stores in that direction.

How an AI tag will get in

Scripts fill everything they can. For the rest, agents never touch the database. They work through a CLI that prints a chapter, checks each answer, and compares two independent runs.

Build order

Each step gives the next ones context, so later questions become choices among things already tagged. Mistakes spread the same way, so the early steps get the most care.

Example visualization

The Book of Mormon's Bible, word by word

Word matches join the Hebrew to the KJV, and the KJV to the Book of Mormon. Chain them and any verse the Book of Mormon quotes reads across three texts at once, with every difference counted instead of spotted by eye. Before, this took two books open side by side.

Where "mercy" comes from

Every Hebrew and Greek word is matched to the KJV words that translate it, so translation choices can be counted. Here are the original words behind eight English words for mercy and grace. One Hebrew word splits into many English words, and many originals collapse into one.
